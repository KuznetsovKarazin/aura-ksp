This research artifact accompanies AURA-KSP, a controlled study of stream reduction for skeleton action recognition on NTU RGB+D 120 XSet. It contains the source code, frozen comparison protocol, four final trained checkpoints, aligned prediction files, original latency traces, exact numerical tables and CPU scripts for independently reproducing the fixed weighted-logit fusion, three aggregate quality endpoints and paired setup-bootstrap analysis.

Removing the bone-motion stream yielded 52,951 correct predictions versus 52,997 for the four-stream reference on 59,477 examples, a difference of -0.077341 percentage points. All three prespecified aggregate non-inferiority criteria were met. The artifact retains class-level changes and the unsuccessful temporal K32 branch. Aggregate non-inferiority does not imply that every class retains its recall.

The previously measured 24.30–24.94% latency reduction used seed-271828 models on an RTX 4090 with batch size 1. The final checkpoints were not retimed. The findings are specific to the investigated system pair and do not establish SOTA performance or generalization across seeds and devices.

Both the repository and research-assets ZIP archives are required for complete reproduction from saved predictions. Extract them as sibling directories. SHA-256 sidecar files accompany the archives. The raw NTU RGB+D dataset is not redistributed and remains subject to its original access conditions.
