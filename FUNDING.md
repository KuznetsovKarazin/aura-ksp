# Funding

This research has been funded by the Science Committee of the Ministry of Science and Higher Education of the Republic of Kazakhstan (Grant No. AP23486538 Research and development of a system for recognizing images in video streams based on artificial intelligence).

The funding statement was supplied by the project collaborators and confirmed by the corresponding author on 20 September 2026. The study evaluates an action-recognition component and its computational efficiency. It does not establish completion of all grant work packages.
